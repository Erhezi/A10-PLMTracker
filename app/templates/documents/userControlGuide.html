Admin User Control

Use this guide as your playbook for reviewing user accounts, approving access, and keeping the Item Management roster accurate. Each section walks through what you see in the User Control table, what to watch for, and how to explain the process to business partners.

Access control

  • One account, access per module. People register once for Item Management. What they can do is set separately for each module (PLM Tracker, Fill/Kill Management) in their row.
  • For admins only. Only administrators can open the User Control page. Anyone else who tries is sent back with a reminder that the area is restricted.
  • Active admins. An admin account must be active to make changes. If an admin is disabled, they cannot reactivate themselves until another admin restores their access.
  • Your own admin access. You can't remove your own admin access; another admin has to. To see what a role experiences, switch off your own module roles, save, and try the module.

Lifecycle actions

Approvals & disables

  • Approve access. Use the green check to activate a pending user. The system timestamps who approved the request and notifies the employee by email so they know they can log in.
  • Disable access. Choose the red “Disable” button to pause an account when someone leaves a project or needs a timeout. You cannot disable your own account, so coordinate with another admin if your access needs to change.
  • Delete user. Remove test or duplicate accounts with the delete option. The user is removed from the table entirely and cannot be restored without re-registering.

Roles

  • Set the whole row, then save. Each row has an Admin box, a PLM Tracker role (pick one: No access, Viewer or Editor) and Fill/Kill Management roles (tick any). Press Save access to apply them together.
  • PLM Viewer vs Editor. Viewers open Groups, Dashboard and Playground, and can filter and export. Editors also use Collect and Conflicts and can change data.
  • Fill/Kill roles. Either Viewer on its own, or any of Sub Finder, Sub Approver and Materials Manager, which can all view as well. Most people are Sub Finder + Sub Approver, or Sub Finder + Materials Manager. One person can't be both Sub Approver and Materials Manager unless they're an admin.
  • Requested modules. When someone registers, they tick the modules they need. User Control shows this as Asked for under their email. It grants nothing until you set their roles and save.
  • Admins. Admin lets someone approve accounts and assign roles here. It gives no access inside a module: an admin needs a module role like anyone else, and can give themselves one in their own row. Clearing deleted rows and archiving completed ones in PLM Tracker need both Admin and the PLM Editor role.

Table interactions

  • Filtering. Use the quick filter to narrow the list by name, email, or role. Separate multiple search terms with semicolons and add * as a wildcard when you only remember part of a word.
  • Sorting. Click any column header to sort by that value. Click again to flip between ascending or descending order. The table remembers which column you last sorted.
  • Row count. A counter above the table shows how many records match your current filters so you can confirm the list looks right before sharing it.
  • Action buttons. Save access, Approve and Disable take effect immediately; Delete asks you to confirm first.
