Fill/Kill Management Docs

How Medline's fill/kill report turns into substitute decisions and orders, and where to do each step.

What it does

Medline sends a fill/kill report every hour from 8am to 11pm. Every order line Medline couldn't fill completely, whether killed outright or only partly filled, is loaded into the module.

The main table rolls those lines up per Infor item for the dates and sites you choose. For each item, nursing (Sub Approver) enters the substitutes that are clinically acceptable, approved on entry: every approved substitute can be ordered; SCM tells the sites how to order it; and each site's Materials Manager adds the ways to order it (its vendor items) and records what they ordered, for their site.

It's where everyone coordinates: nursing's latest comment on each approved substitute and SCM's latest comment on each item ({{ instruction_label }}) read right on the table. Nobody is emailed: open Main, or refresh it, to see the latest. Please record every substitute order here, with its site (and its PO number if you have it), so everyone can see which sites have ordered.

Who does what

Adding each substitute's vendor items, the ways to order it (vendor part, vendor, Infor's vendor ID, UOM, QOE), helped by the search of Infor's active contracts; recording what was ordered from an approved substitute's vendor items, for their site, and adding comments to orders.

AdminAssigns these roles in User Control. Inside the module, admins have only the roles they hold, like everyone else.

Viewer is held on its own; the other roles can view too. Nursing holds Sub Approver, whoever coordinates the sites SCM, and the sites' Materials Managers Materials Manager. Sub Approver and Materials Manager stay with different people, so the person who approves isn't the person who orders (admins excepted).

The pages

Main is where everyone works. Everyone sees the same page; the buttons you get follow your roles.

  • One row per Infor item. Its Medline items, one per UOM (such as each and box), are listed inside the row, each with its QOE (eaches per unit, from Infor), quantities, reason, substitutes, Approved by Nursing (every approved substitute's Reference SKU, each on a line of its own, with a that counts its comments: the latest reads under it, and a click shows them all; Sub Approvers double-click the cell to add a substitute) and Vendor Item Detail: beside each one, a tile for each way to order it, which a Materials Manager clicks to record an order. A Medline item that isn't a Medline vendor item in Infor has a row of its own, marked Not in Infor.
  • {{ instruction_label }}, between the approved substitutes and their vendor items: SCM's latest comment on how the sites should order the item, with who wrote it and when. It stays until they write a new one, whatever dates you look at; lists every one, newest first. SCM double-click the cell to add one, or use Add comment in the item's panel.
  • Choose what to look at: click View today, or Select a range and choose the first and last Fill/Kill dates (the days Medline's killed lines were ordered; it opens on today), choose sites, and type part of an item number or description.
  • Needs filter: Needs subs (no live substitute), Needs review (one is pending review), Needs details (approved, but none has a vendor item yet, so nothing to order: the Materials Manager's to add; for them, a pencil beside each opens the form), Needs order (approved, no order in these dates), Ordered. An item can need more than one thing.
  • Killed against ordered: Total Killed (EA) and Total Sub Ordered (EA), after Ordering, put the Infor item's kills and its substitutes' orders in these dates side by side, in eaches (an order counts its quantity times its QOE). Kill lines keep arriving through the day: when the orders fall short, it says how many eaches, in red, so you can see which items need another order. Each site orders for itself: when an item was killed at several sites, each site that's short says so, and Ordering shows each order's site. With sites chosen, only their kills and orders count.
  • Click an item to open its panel: the Infor item's details, {{ instruction_label }}, the substitutes, the kill lines behind them, the Sub History (every substitute added, reviewed or deleted, and every vendor item added or deleted), and every order, in a table like the kill lines. Click the Infor item to see all its UOMs together, or a Medline item to see only that UOM; the panel switches between the two. Drag the panel's left edge to make it wider or narrower; your browser remembers the width, and a double-click on the edge resets it.
  • Share what you see: the page's address keeps the dates, sites, search, Needs filter and the open item, so copying it gives someone else the same view.
  • Below the table: the orders recorded, with their own dates (the last 30 days unless you change them), so past orders can be looked up without changing the table's dates; they follow the sites chosen above. Each shows its site, Infor item, the vendor part ordered, the QOE, what was ordered, the PO number, and the vendor's ID and name. The search box beside them finds an order by the Medline item, its Infor item, the vendor part, the substitute's Reference SKU, or the PO number.

Subs lists every substitute on file, as a reference, with its Reference SKU and vendor items: search it, filter by status, source, no vendor item yet, or deleted ones, and export what you see as a spreadsheet (a row per vendor item). Click an item to open it on Main.

Dashboard shows trends for the chosen dates and sites (Today, the last 7 or 30 days, all, or dates you choose): kill lines by reason and by site, the most killed items with their substitutes, and how substitutes are being reviewed. It also shows how fast killed items move: the median time from a kill to a substitute, from the substitute to an order, and from the kill to the order. Choose your sites to see their figures, orders and shortfalls included; with every site, it shows the totals.

How an item moves

  1. Killed. The item appears in the main table with its total ordered, killed and filled quantities.
  2. Substitute entered. Nursing gathers options from suppliers (emails, calls) and enters the part number of the one they choose, with a note if useful. It's the Reference SKU, their approved reference: often the manufacturer's part, which several vendors may sell. It stays as entered; the Materials Manager works out how to order it. It's approved right away: approved by nursing means clinically acceptable, and every approved substitute can be ordered. Substitutes stay with the item, so the next time it's killed they're already there.
  3. Proposed substitutes. Medline's backorder substitutes appear automatically (and, later, an AI finder's). They're pending review until a Sub Approver approves, holds or rejects them.
  4. Instructed. SCM writes how the sites should order it: which substitute and vendor item, how much, for which sites. Their latest comment reads on Main for everyone, under {{ instruction_label }}.
  5. Changed later. When nursing no longer wants a substitute ordered, the Sub Approver puts it on Hold or rejects it, with a reason if useful (kept as its comment): it leaves Approved by Nursing and can't be ordered until it's approved again. Its orders so far stay. Click a substitute's History to see every change, with who made it and when.
  6. Ordered. A Materials Manager adds an approved substitute's vendor items, one for each way to order it: a vendor part, its vendor and vendor ID, and a UOM (and QOE, manufacturer part, Infor item, description). The search of Infor's active contracts starts from the Reference SKU and fills one in; for 930103, for example, Medline's B-D930103CSZ (a box of 40) and B-D930103CS (a case of 400) are two vendor items of the same substitute. Then they click a vendor item's tile on Main to record an order, which keeps that vendor item's part, vendor, UOM and QOE. They choose the site it's for (the form starts on your site when you've chosen just that one in the sites above) and, if they have it, Infor's PO number. The form works out what's still needed at that site: the item's eaches killed there less its substitute orders for that site in these dates, and recommends that in whole units of the vendor item, rounded up (1,300 EA still needed, ordered by the CA/1000: 1.3 CA, so 2 CA). The quantity is filled in only when the vendor item has the UOM and QOE that was killed and it comes out whole (30 EA killed, 10 EA ordered: 20 EA); otherwise the form shows its working in red and leaves the quantity to you. Without a QOE, on the vendor item, a UOM killed or an earlier order, it says No QOE in red and recommends nothing; vendor items without one are marked no QOE on Main and in the panel. A UOM typed another way, like CS or VL, is saved as Infor's (CA, VI). Medline's own part for the killed item, from one of Medline's vendor IDs, is the item itself, not a substitute: the form says so and it isn't saved. The vendor ID is required: typing the vendor's name lists Infor's vendors (by their name, legal name or search name) to choose it from. The vendor name always follows the vendor ID: change the ID and Infor's name for it fills in. When Infor doesn't have the vendor, 0000000 (Vendor not available in system) says so: the vendor name then empties for you to type it in. The contract search takes a part number, an Infor item number or words; several words, like 930103 chloraprep, narrow the lines to those having them all. Vendor items stay with the substitute, for the next time; one that's no longer sold can be deleted with a reason. An item can have more than one order. Orders can be corrected, or voided with a reason; both are kept in the order's history.

Good to know

  • Access is per module. If you can't see Fill/Kill Management in the top bar, ask an admin for a Fill/Kill role.
  • An item shows Ordered, with each order's quantity, UOM and date, when an order was recorded within the Fill/Kill dates you chose; otherwise No order. Click it to see all of the item's orders.
  • UOMs. One Infor item can be killed under several Medline items, for example by the each and by the box. They share the Infor item's row, with a last line that adds them up in eaches. A substitute approved for one UOM is flagged on the others; add it there from the item panel and approve it to use it. Its vendor items aren't copied: they're for a UOM.
  • The beside a Medline item means Medline's customer item isn't the Infor item; point at it to see what Medline listed. The Infor item always comes from Infor's Medline vendor items, never from the customer item, which can be the Medline item itself or a contract-only number like 1296-DYNJAA04.
  • Comments are added, never overwritten, and show who wrote them and when.
  • Deleting a substitute asks why. It disappears from the item and the library, but who deleted it, when and why stay in the item's Sub History. Its orders have to be voided first.
  • If someone changed a substitute while you had it open, you'll see who changed it and be asked to reload.
  • Every login ends at 2:30 AM. The next click takes you to the login page, then back to where you were.
