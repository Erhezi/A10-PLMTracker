Fill/Kill Management Docs

How Medline's fill/kill report turns into substitute decisions and orders, and where to do each step.

What it does

Medline sends a fill/kill report every hour from 8am to 11pm. Every order line Medline couldn't fill completely, whether killed outright or only partly filled, is loaded into the module.

The main table rolls those lines up per Infor item for the dates and sites you choose. For each item, nursing (Sub Approver) enters the substitute to use, approved on entry, and a Materials Manager adds the ways to order it (its vendor items) and records what was ordered.

Who does what

RoleResponsible for
ViewerSeeing everything in the module.
Sub ApproverUsually nursing. Entering the Reference SKU of a substitute found through suppliers, with a note if useful: it's approved as soon as it's entered, and picked if it's the item's only approved one. Holding or rejecting substitutes later, reviewing proposed ones, picking and unpicking, adding comments, correcting a Reference SKU, and deleting a substitute with a reason.
Materials ManagerAdding each substitute's vendor items, the ways to order it (vendor part, vendor, Infor's vendor ID, UOM, QOE), helped by the search of Infor's active contracts, and recording what was ordered from the Approver's Pick's vendor items; adding comments to orders.
Sub FinderProposing substitutes, which wait for a Sub Approver's review; adding vendor items. Kept for a future AI agent that finds substitutes.
AdminAssigns these roles in User Control. Inside the module, admins have only the roles they hold, like everyone else.

Viewer is held on its own; the other roles can view too. Nursing holds Sub Approver, and Materials Managers hold Materials Manager. Sub Approver and Materials Manager stay with different people, so the person who approves isn't the person who orders (admins excepted).

The pages

Main is where everyone works. Everyone sees the same page; the buttons you get follow your roles.

  • One row per Infor item. Its Medline items, one per UOM (such as each and box), are listed inside the row, each with its QOE (eaches per unit, from Infor), quantities, reason, substitutes, pick (its Reference SKU) and Vendor Item Detail: a tile for each way to order the pick, which a Materials Manager clicks to record an order. A Medline item that isn't a Medline vendor item in Infor has a row of its own, marked Not in Infor.
  • Choose what to look at: click View today, or Select a range and pick the first and last order dates (it opens on today, or on the latest day with kill lines), pick sites, and type part of an item number or description.
  • Needs filter: Needs subs (no live substitute), Needs review (one is pending review), Needs pick (approved but nothing picked), Needs details (the pick has no vendor item yet, so nothing to order: the Materials Manager's to add; a pencil beside the pick says so), Needs order (picked, no order in these dates), Ordered. An item can need more than one thing.
  • Click an item to open its panel: the Infor item's details, the substitutes, the kill lines behind them, the Sub History (every substitute added, reviewed, picked or unpicked before, and every vendor item added or deleted), and every order. Click the Infor item to see all its UOMs together, or a Medline item to see only that UOM; the panel switches between the two. Drag the panel's left edge to make it wider or narrower; your browser remembers the width, and a double-click on the edge resets it.
  • Share what you see: the page's address keeps the dates, sites, search, Needs filter and the open item, so copying it gives someone else the same view.
  • Below the table: the orders recorded, with their own dates (the last 30 days unless you change them), so past orders can be looked up without changing the table's dates. Each shows its Infor item, the vendor part ordered, the QOE, what was ordered, and the vendor's ID and name. The search box beside them finds an order by the Medline item, its Infor item, the vendor part, or the substitute's Reference SKU.

Subs lists every substitute on file, as a reference, with its Reference SKU and vendor items: search it, filter by status, source, no vendor item yet, or deleted ones, and export what you see as a spreadsheet (a row per vendor item). Click an item to open it on Main.

Dashboard shows trends for the chosen dates and sites: kill lines by reason and by site, the most killed items, how substitutes are being reviewed, and which were rejected. It also shows how fast killed items move: the median time from a kill to a substitute, from the substitute to an order, and from the kill to the order.

How an item moves

  1. Killed. The item appears in the main table with its total ordered, killed and filled quantities.
  2. Substitute entered. Nursing gathers options from suppliers (emails, calls) and enters the part number of the one they choose, with a note if useful. It's the Reference SKU, their approved reference: often the manufacturer's part, which several vendors may sell. It stays as entered; the Materials Manager works out how to order it. It's approved right away, and picked if it's the item's only approved substitute; with several, the Sub Approver picks. Substitutes stay with the item, so the next time it's killed they're already there.
  3. Proposed substitutes. Medline's backorder substitutes appear automatically (and, later, an AI finder's). They're pending review until a Sub Approver approves, holds or rejects them; approving one picks it if it's the only approved one.
  4. Changed later. The Sub Approver can still hold or reject a substitute, pick or unpick, and comment. Leaving approved unpicks it. Click a substitute's History to see every change, with who made it and when.
  5. Ordered. A Materials Manager adds the pick's vendor items, one for each way to order it: a vendor part, its vendor and vendor ID, and a UOM (and QOE, manufacturer part, Infor item, description). The search of Infor's active contracts starts from the Reference SKU and fills one in; for 930103, for example, Medline's B-D930103CSZ (a box of 40) and B-D930103CS (a case of 400) are two vendor items of the same substitute. Then they click a vendor item's tile on Main to record an order, which keeps that vendor item's part, vendor, UOM and QOE. The vendor ID is required: typing the vendor's name lists Infor's vendors (by their name, legal name or search name) to choose it from, and when Infor doesn't have the vendor, 0000000 (Vendor not available in system) says so: the vendor name then empties for you to type it in. The contract search takes a part number, an Infor item number or words; several words, like 930103 chloraprep, narrow the lines to those having them all. Vendor items stay with the substitute, for the next time; one that's no longer sold can be deleted with a reason. An item can have more than one order. Orders can be corrected, or voided with a reason; both are kept in the order's history.

Good to know

  • Access is per module. If you can't see Fill/Kill Management in the top bar, ask an admin for a Fill/Kill role.
  • An item shows Ordered, with each order's quantity, UOM and date, when an order was placed on or after the first of the dates you chose: it was ordered since those kills. Click it to see the orders.
  • UOMs. One Infor item can be killed under several Medline items, for example by the each and by the box. They share the Infor item's row, with a last line that adds them up in eaches. A substitute approved for one UOM is flagged on the others; add it there from the item panel and approve it to use it. Its vendor items aren't copied: they're for a UOM.
  • The beside a Medline item means Medline's customer item isn't the Infor item; point at it to see what Medline listed. The Infor item always comes from Infor's Medline vendor items, never from the customer item, which can be the Medline item itself or a contract-only number like 1296-DYNJAA04.
  • Comments are added, never overwritten, and show who wrote them and when.
  • Deleting a substitute asks why. It disappears from the item and the library, but who deleted it, when and why stay in the item's Sub History. The Approver's Pick has to be unpicked first, and orders voided.
  • If someone changed a substitute while you had it open, you'll see who changed it and be asked to reload.
  • Every login ends at 2:30 AM. The next click takes you to the login page, then back to where you were.
