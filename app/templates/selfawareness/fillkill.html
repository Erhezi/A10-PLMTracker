Fill/Kill Management Docs

How Medline's fill/kill report turns into substitute decisions and orders, and where to do each step.

What it does

Medline sends a fill/kill report every hour from 8am to 11pm. Every order line Medline couldn't fill completely, whether killed outright or only partly filled, is loaded into the module.

The main table rolls those lines up per Medline item for the dates and sites you choose. For each item, Sub Finders record substitutes, a Sub Approver (usually nursing) approves and picks which one to use, and a Materials Manager records what was ordered.

Who does what

RoleResponsible for
ViewerSeeing everything in the module.
Sub FinderAdding substitutes to an item: vendor, vendor part number and comments.
Sub ApproverSetting each substitute to approve, hold or reject, picking the approved one(s) to order, and adding comments to substitutes.
Materials ManagerRecording what was ordered against the Approver's Pick (quantity, UOM, QOE, vendor), and adding comments to orders.
AdminAssigns these roles in User Control. Inside the module, admins have only the roles they hold, like everyone else.

Viewer is held on its own; the other roles can view too. Most people are Sub Finder and Sub Approver, or Sub Finder and Materials Manager. Sub Approver and Materials Manager stay with different people, so the person who approves isn't the person who orders (admins excepted).

The pages

Main is where everyone works. Everyone sees the same page; the buttons you get follow your roles.

  • Choose what to look at: slide or type the order dates (it opens on the latest day), pick sites, and type part of an item number or description.
  • Needs filter: Needs subs (no live substitute), Needs review (one is pending review), Needs pick (approved but nothing picked), Needs order (picked, no order in these dates), Ordered. An item can need more than one thing.
  • Click an item (its Medline item or customer item) to open its panel: its substitutes, the kill lines behind it, everything picked or reviewed before, and every order.
  • Below the table: every substitute on file (the reference library), and the orders recorded in the chosen dates.

Dashboard shows trends for the chosen dates and sites: kill lines by reason and by site, the most killed items, how substitutes are being reviewed, and which were rejected.

How an item moves

  1. Killed. The item appears in the main table with its total ordered, killed and filled quantities.
  2. Substitutes added. Substitutes from Medline's backorder report appear automatically; a Sub Finder adds others, searching Infor's active contracts to fill in the vendor, part number, UOM and QOE. Substitutes stay with the item, so the next time it's killed they're already there.
  3. Reviewed. New substitutes are pending review until a Sub Approver sets them to approve, hold or reject. Click a substitute to see every change, with who made it and when.
  4. Picked. The Sub Approver picks approved substitutes for ordering, usually one per item. A pick stays until it's unpicked or the substitute is no longer approved.
  5. Ordered. A Materials Manager clicks a pick to record the order. An item can have more than one order, for example when one substitute can't cover the whole need. Orders can be corrected, or voided with a reason; both are kept in the order's history.

Good to know

  • Access is per module. If you can't see Fill/Kill Management in the top bar, ask an admin for a Fill/Kill role.
  • An item shows Ordered when an order's date falls inside the dates you chose.
  • Packagings. One Infor item can be killed under several Medline items, for example by the each and by the box. They're listed together under the Infor item, with a header row that adds them up in eaches. A substitute approved for one packaging is flagged on the others; add it there from the item panel and approve it to use it.
  • Comments are added, never overwritten, and show who wrote them and when.
  • If someone changed a substitute while you had it open, you'll see who changed it and be asked to reload.
  • Every login ends at 2:30 AM. The next click takes you to the login page, then back to where you were.
