Fill/Kill Management Docs

How Medline's fill/kill report turns into substitute decisions and orders. The module is being built; this page describes how it will work, and will grow with it.

What it does

Medline sends a fill/kill report every hour from 8am to 11pm. Every order line Medline couldn't fill completely, whether killed outright or only partly filled, is loaded into the module.

The main table rolls those lines up per Medline item for the dates and sites you choose. For each item, Sub Finders record substitutes, a Sub Approver (usually nursing) approves and picks which one to use, and a Materials Manager records what was ordered.

Who does what

RoleResponsible for
ViewerSeeing everything in the module.
Sub FinderAdding substitutes to an item: vendor, vendor part number and comments.
Sub ApproverSetting each substitute to approve, wait or reject, and picking the approved one(s) to order.
Materials ManagerRecording what was ordered against the Approver's Pick: quantity, UOM, vendor and comments. Can also add substitutes.
AdminAssigns these roles in User Control. Inside the module, admins have only the roles they hold, like everyone else.

Viewer is held on its own; the other roles can view too. One person can be Sub Finder and Sub Approver, or Sub Finder and Materials Manager. Sub Approver and Materials Manager stay with different people, so the person who approves isn't the person who orders (admins excepted).

How an item moves

  1. Killed. The item appears in the main table with its total ordered, killed and filled quantities.
  2. Substitutes added. Substitutes from Medline's backorder report appear automatically; a Sub Finder or Materials Manager adds others. Substitutes stay with the item, so the next time it's killed they're already there.
  3. Reviewed. A Sub Approver sets each substitute to approve, wait or reject. Click a substitute to see every change, with who made it and when.
  4. Picked. The Sub Approver picks approved substitutes for ordering, usually one per item.
  5. Ordered. A Materials Manager clicks a pick to record the order. An item can have more than one order, for example when one substitute can't cover the whole need.

Medline's kill reasons

Comment on the reportWhat it means
Allocation limit has been reachedMedline is rationing the item.
MBO – ERD is dateManufacturer backorder, with an expected release date.
OUT OF STOCK – ETA is dateMedline's warehouse is out, with an expected arrival date.
SPIKE MBO – Forecast x, Usage yOrdered above forecast, during a manufacturer backorder.
NONSTOCK UOM – item stocked by CS/BXOrdered in a unit Medline doesn't stock for this account.
NONSTOCK – needs a stock requestNot stocked on fill/kill accounts.
DISCONTINUED ITEMNo longer made.

Good to know

  • Access is per module. If you can't see Fill/Kill Management in the top bar, ask an admin for a Fill/Kill role.
  • Every login ends at 2:30 AM. The next click takes you to the login page, then back to where you were.
