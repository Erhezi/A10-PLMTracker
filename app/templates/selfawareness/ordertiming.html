Order Timing Docs

Did Medline get our orders, and did it confirm them?

What it does

Medline sends a data feed of the EDI orders it received from us, and what it has done with each line since. Order Timing shows that feed. It's for looking only: nothing is entered here.

  • Main lists our orders, newest first: one row per PO, with its PO type, when Medline received it, when Medline confirmed it, its total lines, how many are in each status and how many are short shipped. Click a row to see its lines: each with its line type, its manufacturer and manufacturer part number, what it's short, and, when Medline flags it as a bad line, Medline's reject code.
  • Short Ships lists the items Medline short shipped, one row per item, with how many times Fill/Kill shows the item killed today, in the last 7 days and in the last 30. See below.
  • The Dashboard gives the whole picture in the days and sites chosen: how many POs Medline got, how fast it confirmed them, when they come in and which got their ASN the same day, and what it rejected or short shipped, site by site. See below.

Finding an order

  • Find an order looks in the PO, Medline's order number, the item numbers (Medline's, ours and the manufacturer's), the description, the ship-to and the buyer. Every word must match, so "nitrile glove" finds nitrile gloves.
  • To check several POs at once, paste them in, separated by spaces, commas or new lines: each one found is listed.
  • Item numbers match without dashes, dots or spaces: "bd930103" finds B-D930103.
  • Order days are the days Medline received the orders. Main and Short Ships open on today, and look back 30 days at most, and not before 11/16/2026, when Medline's LUM orders went live. When a search finds nothing, Search from looks as far back as it goes.
  • Orders are listed 20 to a page, newest first. The page buttons at the top of the table move between them.
  • The tabs above the table list every order, or only the orders with a line in one status (Rejected lists every order with a rejected line), or the orders with a short-shipped line (Short shipped). Each tab shows how many orders it has in the days, sites and search chosen. An order with lines in two statuses is under both tabs, so the tabs don't add up to All orders. Click an order to see its lines: the tab's lines come first, highlighted.
  • When the search or a status finds some lines of an order, those lines are highlighted.
  • Export CSV downloads every line the filters find, on every page, as a spreadsheet.

If a PO you sent isn't found, Medline hasn't reported receiving it as of the time shown at the top right (Data as of: when Medline made the file we loaded, in New York time). Medline sends a file through the day and we load each one within the hour (last loaded), so a PO sent in the last hour or two may not be there yet. Between 8 AM and 11 PM, the load turns into a yellow warning when it's more than 90 minutes ago, as on Fill/Kill's Main.

What the statuses mean

An order is Confirmed when Medline has sent its order confirmation (EDI 855) for it. Each line then moves on by itself:

Not confirmed
Medline received the line but hasn't confirmed it yet.
Rejected
Medline rejected the line, and it won't be filled. Medline marks it as a bad line, and its reject code is shown under Rej code (for example E1, often with the Medline item shown as INVALID PART).
Confirmed
Confirmed, and not shipped yet.
ASN sent
Medline sent the advance ship notice (EDI 856): it has left Medline's warehouse.

Short shipped isn't a status: a confirmed line and a line with its ASN sent can both be short. It means Medline is shipping fewer than were ordered (less any still open). Most of our lines are FILL-KILL lines: Medline ships what it has and kills the rest, so these lines also show up in Fill/Kill. A PO's Short shipped column counts its short lines, and each line's Short says how many it's short.

Times are Medline's, as Medline sends them.

Short Ships

One row per Medline item with a short-shipped line in the days, sites and search chosen. It's for spotting the items Medline keeps killing.

  • Short lines counts the item's short-shipped lines and the POs they're on; Qty short adds up what they're short and Qty ordered what was ordered on them, in the item's unit of measure, with the same in eaches below. QOE is how many eaches one of that unit holds, from Infor (one for each unit when the item's lines are in several; then the quantities are in eaches). A dash means Infor has none, and then there are no eaches.
  • Killed today, 7 days and 30 days count PO lines, not quantities: the item's lines in Medline's Fill/Kill feed (the one Fill/Kill Management shows), each a PO line Medline killed all or part of, today included, at the sites chosen. They don't follow the order days. The page opens with the items killed most in 30 days at the top; click a header to sort by it.
  • Click an item to see its short lines: the PO, the PO line #, line type, when the PO was created, its total lines, site, what was ordered, shipped and short, and Medline's reason from its Fill/Kill feed with its estimated available date. "Not in Fill/Kill yet" means Fill/Kill's feed hasn't loaded that line yet: it loads on its own schedule.
  • Fill/Kill opens the item on Fill/Kill's Main, with its last 30 days.
  • Export CSV downloads every short-shipped line the filters find, each with its QOE, its quantities in eaches and its item's kill counts.

The Dashboard

The whole of Medline's order feed in the order days and sites chosen. It opens on the last 7 days, and reaches as far back as Main. A PO's time to confirm runs from when Medline received it to its 855, the same minutes Main shows.

  • The figures across the top: POs and lines, the share confirmed, the median time to confirm, the share with an ASN the same day, the median time from receipt to ASN, and the rejected and short-shipped lines. The links open those POs on Main, or their items on Short Ships, in the same days and sites.
  • Lines per day, by status: each day's lines in each status. Hover a day for its short-shipped lines.
  • When POs come in, and how fast Medline confirms them: POs by the hour Medline received them, shaded by how long it took to confirm them. POs received late in the evening can wait until Medline creates their orders early the next morning, five hours or more.
  • Which POs get their ASN the same day: POs by the hour Medline created the order, with an ASN that day or not. Medline's report covers each order day only, so an ASN sent the next day never shows up in this feed: a PO without one may well have shipped the day after.
  • Slowest POs to confirm: click a PO to open it on Main.
  • By site: the same figures for each site, with all sites at the bottom.
  • Rejected lines: by Medline's reject code and item, as Medline wrote the item and as our PO had it.

Hours are on Medline's clock, as Medline sends its times.

Who can see it

Everyone with a Fill/Kill Management role, whichever it is. Order Timing has no roles of its own: to get access, ask an admin for a Fill/Kill role.
