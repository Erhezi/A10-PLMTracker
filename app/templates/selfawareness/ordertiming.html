Order Timing Docs

When Medline receives our orders and fills them through the day.

What it does

Medline sends a data feed of the EDI orders it received from us, which it fills through the day. Order Timing will show that feed: Main for the orders themselves, and the Dashboard for trends. It's for looking only: nothing is entered here.

That data isn't ready yet, so both pages are empty for now.

Who can see it

Everyone with a Fill/Kill Management role, whichever it is. Order Timing has no roles of its own: to get access, ask an admin for a Fill/Kill role.
